01 / About

Not interested in building technology just because it can be built.

I am interested in the layer beneath the application: the systems, infrastructure and constraints that determine whether technology is actually usable.

My work has increasingly moved toward questions around local AI, resilient computing, Linux, cybersecurity and what it means to design technology for African environments instead of assuming permanent cloud access, unlimited bandwidth or expensive hardware.

I am currently studying Computer Science at the University of Zimbabwe while building YorOS and continuing to deepen my technical foundation across software, operating systems and AI.

02 / Selected work

Building from the infrastructure layer upward.

Selected work only — focused on the projects that best represent where I am going.

Flagship2026 — Present

YorOS

An offline-first AI operating environment being built for African education.

YorOS explores what happens when AI is designed into the computing environment itself rather than added as another cloud-dependent application. The project combines a Linux base, local AI and education-focused tools with an emphasis on limited-connectivity environments and practical hardware.
