Project

Scorexi Football

A football product built around live match information, standings, news, highlights and fan-facing experiences.

RoleBuilder
AreaSports technology
Platforms exploredWeb · Mobile

The idea

Scorexi was developed as a football experience that brought together live, finished and scheduled matches, match detail, standings, football news and highlights in one product.

The project also explored how fan engagement could go beyond passive score checking through additional match-centred experiences.

What I worked on

  • Live, finished and scheduled match views.
  • Match detail and league standings.
  • News and highlights integration.
  • Football-data API integration.
  • Web and mobile product experimentation.
  • An SX5 fantasy concept built around selecting five players for a match.
