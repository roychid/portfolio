Project

PoultrySmart

An AI-and-agriculture project exploring how software intelligence can support poultry operations and decision-making.

RoleLead Innovator
AreaAI · AgriTech
ContextZimbabwe

The project

PoultrySmart explored the application of artificial intelligence to poultry management and farm decision support. The project was part of my broader experimentation with building technology around practical African industry problems.

This portfolio keeps the description high-level rather than presenting unverified commercial traction or capabilities that were not deployed in production.

What it represents

  • Exploration of AI in an agricultural operating context.
  • Product thinking around data-informed farm decisions.
  • Experience translating an industry problem into a technical concept.
  • Early-stage innovation work rather than a claimed scaled commercial product.
