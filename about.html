About me

I’m Roy Chidhungwana.

I am currently pursuing Computer Science at the University of Zimbabwe while actively exploring defensive cybersecurity, Linux, artificial intelligence and systems engineering. I’m also keen on robotics and the intersection between intelligent software and real-world systems.

What I’m learning

I’m focused on developing strong foundations in software engineering and understanding how secure, resilient systems are designed from the infrastructure layer upward.

My current interests include defensive cybersecurity, Linux, AI, systems engineering and local computing. I’m especially interested in how these areas connect rather than treating them as isolated disciplines.

How I build

I learn best by building. Projects give me a way to move from theory into architecture, implementation, testing and iteration. I’m increasingly interested in technically difficult problems where software has to work within real infrastructure constraints.
